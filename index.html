Our Story

Made by hand, made to last.

EG Microphones was born out of frustration. We (Joe Esposito and Dejan Gatt) were tired of paying premium prices for microphones built with cheap capsules. So we started building their own. What began as a workshop project became a company which is passionate to makeing studio quality audio accessible.
